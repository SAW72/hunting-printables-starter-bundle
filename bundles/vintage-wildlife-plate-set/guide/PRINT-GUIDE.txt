Vintage Wildlife Plate Set — print guide
Steward of the King LLC · hello@stewardoftheking.com

What's inside
10 naturalist plates: buck, doe, mallard, turkey, fox, bear, GSP, brook trout, Canada goose, antler still life.

How to print
1. Open the PNG (or JPG master) in any print dialog or send to a print shop.
2. These are portrait 3:4 plates (864 × 1152 px). They print cleanly at 5×7. For 8×10, choose "fit to page" and expect a small cream border or a slight crop.
3. Print on warm cream or off-white cardstock if you can. White paper works; cream looks closer to the plate.
4. Frame with a simple wood or black frame. Gallery wall: hang as a set of 3, 6, or all 10.
