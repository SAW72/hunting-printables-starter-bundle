What's inside
10 quote cards as SVG (true vector). Print at 8×10, 11×14, 16×20, or A4 without losing sharpness.

How to print
1. Open the SVG in a browser, Preview, or Illustrator. Print, or export to PDF/PNG at the size you want.
2. Designed at 8×10 inches. Scale up freely — it is vector.
3. Cream / ivory paper looks best. Black ink on kraft also works.
4. Hang in a mudroom, kitchen, cabin, or as a gift from wife to husband (or the other way).
